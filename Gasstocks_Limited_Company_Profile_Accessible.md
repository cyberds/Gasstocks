## Text

Nigeria is reported to be the world's eight oil producing nation with an average production of 2million barrels of oil per day. The exploration and production activities associated with this resource accounts for 90% of the country's annual national income. About 70% of Nigeria's crude oil and gas production is in the Niger Delta region which is in the southern part of Nigeria. It is one of the world's largest arcuate fan-shaped river deltas. The riverine area of the Niger Delta is a coastal belt of swamps bordering the Atlantic Ocean. The vegetation of the Niger Delta consists mainly of forest swamps of reticulate pattern of interconnected meandering creeks and distributaries of the River Niger. These riverine communities are also home to an estimated population of about 12,24million people. Who live mainly in small villages scattered along the banks of rivers and creeks.

The oil exported from this region to the United States of America and the Asian continent has increased international attention on Nigeria. To the United States it is a key supplier and a critical player in its oil security plan, while for the Asian continent it continues to enhance the balance of trade it has with Nigeria. The implication of this development is that Nigeria will continue to enjoy the support of foreign government to ensure continuous exploration and production activities. Local and international conscious security efforts are constantly being deployed to ensure the security and peace of the region is not compromised. To the extent that it experiences minimum obstructions as not to threaten or distort future oil security plan. To promote sustainable development in the oil producing Niger Delta region in the face of violent agitation for resource control by host communities, the participation of indigenous companies in Nigeria's oil and gas industry is being encouraged.

This has resulted in a continuous growth in the number of local companies playing in the oil and gas industry. Associated with this development is the challenge of low-quality service delivery making it difficult for them to do business with International Oil Companies. This has opened greater opportunities to indigenous companies focused on providing efficient and outstanding services.

One of the few Nigerian company taking advantage of these available opportunity and providing excellent services which are adaptive to the Niger Delta and existing OCs, is Gasstocks Limited.
